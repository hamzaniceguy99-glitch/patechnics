Terms of service

What we provide

PA Technics provides electrical installation and charging infrastructure work carried out by PA TECHNICS, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0803.036.670. CAPACITY-BASED DISTRIBUTION CHARGES: since 1 January 2023 a component of the distribution tariff applicable in the Flemish Region to customers equipped with a digital meter is determined by reference to the monthly peak, being the highest quarter-hourly average offtake recorded in the month, averaged over the preceding twelve months and subject to a floor. The consequence, which is explained to every client before a charge point is specified, is that a single period of high simultaneous offtake affects the capacity component for a rolling twelve-month period. No figure is given for any saving, because the amount payable depends on the applicable tariff, on the client's own supply contract and on the client's own pattern of use, none of which this company controls. RESIDUAL CURRENT PROTECTION: equipment for charging electric vehicles is capable of producing smooth direct residual current which may impair the operation of a residual current device of type AC or type A and prevent it from operating. Protection of a charging circuit is accordingly provided either by a residual current device of type B or by a device of type A in combination with equipment providing detection of smooth direct residual current at six milliamps, and the charging circuit is a dedicated circuit. INSPECTION BEFORE COMMISSIONING: a new or modified electrical installation is subject to inspection by an accredited body before it is put into service, in accordance with the general regulations on electrical installations, and the installation is not commissioned until that inspection has taken place. CONNECTION CAPACITY: an increase in connection capacity, or conversion to a three-phase connection, requires an application to the distribution network operator. The timescale and the cost of such an application are determined by the network operator and not by this company, and no warranty is given as to either. The available power is confirmed before any charging power is promised. VEHICLE COMPONENTS: the company does not place electrical or electronic equipment for motor vehicles on the market under its own name or trade mark, and accordingly makes no declaration of conformity and bears no obligations as a manufacturer of such equipment. NO WARRANTY OF SAVINGS OR OUTCOME: no representation is made as to the amount of any reduction in a client's bill, which depends on tariffs, supply contracts and behaviour, nor as to charging times, which depend on the vehicle, its on-board charger, its state of charge and ambient conditions. The company does not provide legal advice, which is reserved to the Bar, nor accountancy or tax services, which are reserved to professionals registered with the ITAA. Services are described on the Services page and indicative prices on the home page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
