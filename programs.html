Program 01 · Dedicated and protected

Charge point installation

Two things get specified before anything else, and the second is the one nobody asks about. The circuit is dedicated, because a charger drawing its maximum for hours is not a kettle. And the residual current protection has to cope with smooth DC residual current, which can stop an ordinary device operating at all: that means type B, or type A together with six milliamp DC detection in the charger itself. A charger on a shared circuit behind a standard device is cheaper and is not the same installation.

Program 02 · Where the cost lives

Load management and peaks

A charger that simply takes its rated power whenever a car is plugged in will, sooner or later, do it at the worst possible moment. Dynamic load balancing measures what the rest of the building is drawing and gives the car what is left, which is slower on the few occasions it matters and invisible the rest of the time. Static limiting is a blunter version of the same idea and still better than nothing. Neither of them is a comfort feature.

Program 03 · Codes 43.211 and 43.240

Electrical installation

Most of the avoidable problems in a small installation come from a board that has been extended four times by four people, with no record of what feeds what. So the work starts with establishing that, in writing, and a client ends up with a schedule they can hand to the next person. New or altered work is not commissioned until it has been inspected by an accredited body, which is a legal step and not a formality we can waive for anyone.

Program 04 · Code 42.220

Connection capacity

A great many charging plans fail on a fact that could have been established on day one: the connection will not carry it. Going from single-phase to three-phase, or raising the connection capacity, means an application to the distribution network operator, which sets both the cost and the timescale. Neither is ours to promise. What we can do is find out first, tell you plainly, and design around what exists if waiting is not acceptable.
